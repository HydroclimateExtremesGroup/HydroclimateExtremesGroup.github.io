Research Interests

Wentao's research focuses on statistical methods and machine learning applications in hydrology and climate science. His work involves developing novel approaches for precipitation estimation and uncertainty quantification, particularly in the context of satellite-based precipitation products and ensemble prediction systems.

Biography

Wentao Zhan is a Postdoctoral Researcher jointly advised by Dr. Daniel Wright and Dr. Matthias Katzfuss. His research combines statistical methods with hydrological applications, focusing on improving precipitation estimation and uncertainty quantification through innovative data science approaches.
